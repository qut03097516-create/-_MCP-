### [Premier Energies Commissions 7GW TOPCon Cell Plant in India](https://www.pv-tech.org/premier-energies-commissions-7gw-topcon-cell-plant-takes-capacity-to-10-6gw/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Indian manufacturer Premier Energies has commissioned a 7GW n-type TOPCon G12R solar cell manufacturing facility in Naidupeta, Andhra Pradesh, increasing its total solar cell capacity to 10.6GW. The plant was developed with a capital expenditure of INR 32.93 billion \(US$343.6 million\) and can produce approximately 88,000 cells per hour, marking it as India&\#x27;s largest solar cell manufacturing site now in trial production. This expansion is significant as it provides scale to meet rising demand with improved supply reliability and supports India&\#x27;s clean energy transition as part of the company&\#x27;s integrated manufacturing strategy. The facility uses digital manufacturing, AI-based tools for process control, and automation, while targeting average cell efficiencies of around 25.8% after ramp-up. It also supports upgrades to next-generation TOPCon+ technologies and includes a Zero Liquid Discharge system for water conservation.

rss · PV-Tech · Sep 21, 10:35

**「Technical Details」** The plant produces n-type TOPCon \(Tunnel Oxide Passivated Contact\) G12R solar cells, which are designed for higher efficiency and better temperature performance compared to p-type cells. It incorporates AI-based predictive analysis, automated material transport, and precision manufacturing to enhance production consistency and throughput. The facility is engineered for upgrades to TOPCon+ technologies, such as poly-finger metallization and advanced edge-isolation processes, with a target of achieving average cell efficiencies of approximately 25.8% following stabilization and ramp-up.

**「Industry Impact」** The commissioning significantly bolsters India&\#x27;s solar manufacturing capacity, reducing import dependency and enhancing supply chain resilience for high-efficiency solar products in both domestic and international markets.

### [Court Restores $7 Billion Solar for All Program for Low-Income Households](https://www.pv-tech.org/us-judge-overturns-epa-cancellation-of-us7-billion-solar-for-all-programme/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

A US federal judge ruled that the Environmental Protection Agency \(EPA\) unlawfully terminated the $7 billion Solar for All programme, ordering its reinstatement. This programme, established under the Inflation Reduction Act in 2024, was designed to expand solar access and reduce electricity costs for low- and moderate-income households, with an estimated potential to reach over 900,000 households and support approximately 200,000 jobs. The EPA cancelled the programme in August 2025 following the passage of the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, but the court determined the agency lacked authority to terminate grants that had already been obligated. The ruling is part of broader legal challenges to federal clean-energy funding withdrawals and restores critical resources for solar deployment in underserved markets.

**「Industry Impact」** The reinstatement of the Solar for All programme could significantly accelerate solar installations in low-income communities and bolster job creation in the solar sector, as evidenced by EPA estimates of reaching 900,000 households and supporting 200,000 jobs.

### [JA&\#x27;s HJT Solar Modules Begin First In-Orbit Space Test](https://www.pv-tech.org/ja-hjt-solar-module-space-launch/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

JA Solar launched prototype p-type heterojunction \(P-HJT\) solar modules into space on September 17 via a Kuaizhou-11 rocket from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Centre, marking the first in-orbit test of such technology. This mission aims to assess the long-term reliability and degradation of P-HJT modules in low-Earth orbit by comparing in-orbit performance data with ground-based simulations. Space PV has traditionally relied on costly gallium-arsenide, and successful validation could position crystalline silicon technologies as a more cost-effective alternative for satellite power systems. JA emphasized that this is an early exploration phase with no current orders or material impact on operations.

rss · PV-Tech · Sep 21, 10:31

**「Technical Details」** The P-HJT modules are designed to withstand space conditions, including radiation exposure and extreme temperature variations, which are key challenges for silicon-based photovoltaics in harsh environments. JA will monitor in-orbit performance to validate degradation models and evaluate the technology&\#x27;s suitability for sustained use in low-Earth orbit, where traditional gallium-arsenide dominates due to its durability.

**「Industry Impact」** If validated, this test could accelerate the adoption of cost-effective crystalline silicon PV in space applications, potentially lowering satellite power costs and supporting the expanding space economy, including areas like communications and remote sensing. However, JA notes that large-scale commercialization remains highly uncertain, with space PV still in an early exploratory stage.

### [Dilip Buildcon divests 1.36GW Indian solar portfolio](https://www.pv-tech.org/dilip-buildcon-divests-stake-in-1-36gw-solar-pv-portfolio/) ⭐️ 5.0/10

Indian infrastructure firm Dilip Buildcon \(DBL\) has signed definitive agreements to divest its stake in a 1.36GWac solar PV portfolio in Madhya Pradesh to Alpha Alternatives. The portfolio, spread across 163 locations and targeting commercial operations by September 2027, has an estimated project cost of INR68.29 billion \(US$712.8 million\), with equity funding split 51:49 between DBL&\#x27;s subsidiary and Alpha Alternatives during construction. The projects are being developed under power purchase agreements with the Madhya Pradesh Power Management Company as part of the PM-KUSUM scheme. This transaction supports DBL&\#x27;s &\#x27;DBL 2.0&\#x27; asset-light strategy to recycle capital and strengthen its balance sheet.

**「Industry Impact」** This divestment exemplifies ongoing asset recycling in India&\#x27;s solar market, allowing developers like DBL to recycle capital and redeploy it into new projects, thereby supporting the sector&\#x27;s growth and the state of Madhya Pradesh&\#x27;s ambitious renewable energy targets.
