### [Poland Schedules 64TWh Solar PV and Wind Auction for November 2026](https://www.pv-tech.org/poland-set-to-launch-64twh-solar-pv-auction-in-november/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Poland&\#x27;s Energy Regulatory Office has scheduled a solar PV and onshore wind auction for 9-10 November 2026, seeking a combined 64TWh of energy capacity. The first day is dedicated to new projects up to 1MW with 11.25TWh sought, while the second targets projects above 1MW with 53.25TWh sought. This tender is backed by substantial funding of PLN3.8 billion \(US$1 billion\) and PLN14.1 billion for each respective category, forming part of a larger government-supported auction totaling 76.1TWh of capacity with a PLN24.9 billion fund. Poland&\#x27;s rapid solar expansion, with over 20GW installed between 2020 and 2025, and the dominance of solar in last year&\#x27;s auction results underscore the market&\#x27;s significance.

rss · PV-Tech · Sep 21, 10:36

**「Technical Details」** The auction is structured into two segments based on project capacity: small-scale projects of up to 1MW and utility-scale projects above 1MW. This segmentation is typical in renewable energy auctions to accommodate different technology scales and development complexities. Last year&\#x27;s tender for projects above 1MW had a reference price of PLN389/MWh for solar PV, with awarded bids ranging between PLN216.9/MWh and PLN329.68/MWh, indicating competitive pricing and potential cost-reduction trends.

**「Industry Impact」** The auction is poised to drive significant new solar PV investment in Poland, reinforcing its position as one of Europe&\#x27;s fastest-growing solar markets. The high allocation of capacity and funding to solar PV specifically, following last year&\#x27;s results where solar won 126 of 129 bids, signals strong continued policy support and market confidence in the technology within the region.

### [Premier Energies Commissions 7GW TOPCon Cell Plant, Total Capacity Reaches 10.6GW](https://www.pv-tech.org/premier-energies-commissions-7gw-topcon-cell-plant-takes-capacity-to-10-6gw/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Indian manufacturer Premier Energies has commissioned a 7GW TOPCon solar cell facility in Naidupeta, Andhra Pradesh, increasing its total solar cell manufacturing capacity to 10.6GW. The plant, developed with an investment of US$343.6 million, is India&\#x27;s largest and targets average cell efficiencies of 25.8% after stabilization. It incorporates digital manufacturing, AI-based predictive analysis, and automated systems to enhance throughput and consistency. This expansion is part of Premier Energies&\#x27; strategy to build an integrated manufacturing platform and support India&\#x27;s clean energy goals.

rss · PV-Tech · Sep 21, 10:35

**「Technical Details」** The facility produces n-type TOPCon cells using G12R technology, with designs for future upgrades to TOPCon+ variants including poly-finger metallization and advanced edge isolation. AI-driven tools for predictive performance analysis and process control, along with automated material transport systems, enable a production rate of approximately 88,000 cells per hour. The plant&\#x27;s architecture is optimized for scalability and integration of next-generation technologies to achieve higher cell efficiencies.

**「Industry Impact」** Premier Energies&\#x27; expansion substantially increases domestic solar cell production capacity in India, reducing import dependence and strengthening the local photovoltaic supply chain.

### [JA Solar Launches HJT Modules for In-Orbit Space Test](https://www.pv-tech.org/ja-hjt-solar-module-space-launch/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

JA Solar has successfully launched prototype p-type heterojunction \(P-HJT\) photovoltaic modules into orbit aboard a Kuaizhou-11 rocket from Jiuquan Satellite Launch Centre on 17 September, marking the first in-orbit test of this technology. The mission aims to assess the long-term reliability and degradation of P-HJT modules in low-Earth orbit by comparing in-orbit performance data with ground-based simulations. This test could potentially open the field for cost-effective crystalline silicon alternatives to gallium-arsenide in space applications, where cost-efficiency is increasingly crucial due to anticipated growth in the space economy. However, JA Solar cautions that space PV remains at an early stage of exploration, with no current orders or material impact on its operating performance.

rss · PV-Tech · Sep 21, 10:31

**「Industry Impact」** If validation targets are achieved, this program could accelerate the adoption of cost-competitive crystalline silicon modules in low-Earth orbit applications, challenging the dominance of gallium-arsenide and potentially lowering satellite power system costs. However, JA Solar notes that large-scale commercialization of space PV remains highly uncertain.

### [Madison Energy Launches 1GW DER Initiative for AI and Community](https://www.pv-tech.org/madison-energy-launches-1gw-der-community-initiative/) ⭐️ 5.0/10

Madison Energy Infrastructure has launched a 1GW distributed energy resource \(DER\) initiative called Community Infrastructure, aiming to mobilize up to US$2 billion in capital by 2028. The initiative targets the growing power demand from artificial intelligence while delivering lasting benefits to communities, leveraging an existing ecosystem of over 600 projects across more than 30 US states. Projects in progress include standalone energy storage, solar-plus-storage, and solar PV installations with partners such as CORE Electric Cooperative and Denver International Airport. The company states that DER can complement utility-scale generation by being faster, cheaper, and closer to consumption points, addressing local concerns about electricity prices and resource constraints.

**「Industry Impact」** This initiative underscores the growing role of distributed energy resources in powering data centers, potentially accelerating DER market growth for AI applications and influencing developer strategies toward community-integrated projects.
