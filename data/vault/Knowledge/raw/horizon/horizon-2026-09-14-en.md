### [US finalises AD/CVD duties up to 249.13% on solar cells from India, Indonesia, Laos](https://www.pv-tech.org/us-finalises-ad-cvd-duties-on-solar-cell-imports-from-india-indonesia-and-laos/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

The U.S. Department of Commerce finalised antidumping and countervailing duties on crystalline silicon PV cells from India, Indonesia and Laos on 11 September, with combined rates reaching 249.13% for Indian producers before other tariffs are applied. For India, the final dumping margin is 123.04% and the CVD rate is 126.09%, while Indonesia faces a 94.36% dumping margin and CVD rates between 73.20% and 173.70%, and Laos faces a 65.43% dumping margin and CVD rates between 82.03% and 153.67%. These determinations cover crystalline silicon PV cells whether or not assembled into modules, but they do not become permanent orders unless the U.S. International Trade Commission affirms material injury to domestic producers in its final determination scheduled for 14 October 2026. If the ITC rules affirmatively, Commerce will issue final duty orders on 2 November 2026; otherwise, collected cash deposits will be refunded.

rss · PV-Tech · Sep 14, 12:11

**「Technical details」** The duties apply to crystalline silicon photovoltaic cells, the semiconductor devices that convert sunlight into electricity, regardless of whether they are imported as bare cells or already assembled into modules. The final rates combine antidumping margins, which remedy below-fair-value pricing, with countervailing duties, which offset foreign government subsidies, and cash deposit rates are adjusted for subsidy offsets to prevent double counting.

**「Industry impact」** The finalised duties are expected to halt crystalline silicon cell imports from Indonesia and Laos and severely restrict Indian shipments, pressuring manufacturers to serve European markets or relocate production to countries such as Egypt and Ethiopia. An affirmative ITC injury determination on 14 October 2026 is required for the orders to become permanent, though many Indian producers had already minimized U.S. exports over the past year.

### [Spain launches 937MW renewables and storage auction in Teruel](https://www.pv-tech.org/spanish-government-seeks-937mw-renewables-and-energy-storage-in-teruel/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Spain’s Ministry of Ecological Transition \(MITECO\) has opened a 937MW grid access auction for renewable energy and energy storage projects to be connected to the Mudéjar 400 kV substation in Teruel, northeastern Spain. The auction is designed to replace more than 1GW of decommissioned lignite-fired generation capacity at the same location, with awards prioritized for more advanced projects that meet socioeconomic criteria. For each megawatt awarded, developers must hire at least one full-time worker, train one person for a minimum of 60 hours, install 2kW of self-consumption capacity for local users, and invest €10,000 \(US$11,600\) in the Teruel value chain, all backed by a guarantee of €120 per installed kilowatt. Applications will be processed in the order received to favor mature projects, and the submission window remains open until 22 September 2026.

rss · PV-Tech · Sep 14, 10:16

**「Technical details」** The auction mandates that winning projects connect to the high-voltage Mudéjar substation at 400 kV, integrating variable renewable generation with energy storage to compensate for the loss of synchronous lignite baseload capacity. Developers must also pair large-scale capacity with distributed self-consumption, specifically 2 kW of local user self-consumption capacity for every 1 MW of awarded grid access, embedding distributed resources within the centralized replacement framework.

**「Industry impact」** The Teruel auction creates a 937MW grid-access opportunity for renewables-plus-storage developers but imposes stringent local socioeconomic obligations—including a €120/kW guarantee and €10,000/MW local investment requirement—that may favor larger, more established market participants.

### [CrossBoundary Energy commissions 233MW solar-plus-storage plant at DRC copper mine](https://www.pv-tech.org/crossboundary-energy-powers-up-233mw-solar-plus-storage-site-at-drc-copper-mine/) ⭐️ 5.0/10

CrossBoundary Energy has commenced commercial operations at a 233MW/526MWh solar-plus-storage facility adjacent to the Kamoa-Kakula copper mine in the Democratic Republic of the Congo under a power purchase agreement that supplies round-the-clock electricity to support mine expansion. The project was reportedly built within 16 months of PPA execution, a timeline CrossBoundary called the fastest for a large-scale solar and battery energy storage system project in Africa. Approximately 360,000 solar modules from Chinese manufacturer Aiko were deployed, comprising 655W dual-glass ABC units with a stated conversion efficiency of 24.2 percent.

rss · PV-Tech · Sep 14, 10:30

**「Technical details」** The array uses Aiko’s all-back-contact ABC cell architecture, which relocates front-side metallization to the rear surface to minimize shading losses and enable higher efficiencies than conventional cell designs. These modules are paired with a 526MWh battery energy storage system to store daytime solar generation and discharge it continuously, while their improved temperature coefficient and hotspot suppression characteristics are engineered to reduce thermal degradation and safety risks in the DRC’s high-ambient-temperature operating conditions.

**「Industry impact」** The plant illustrates a broader shift in African mining procurement, where solar-plus-storage is being contracted as baseload to displace diesel and fossil generation, a trend underscored by Ember data showing that DRC battery imports have recently exceeded solar imports by dollar value to supply similar remote mining projects.
