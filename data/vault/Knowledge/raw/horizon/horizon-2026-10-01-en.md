### [Tongwei Launches 490W TOPCon Residential Module with 24.5% Efficiency](https://www.pv-tech.org/tongwei-tnc-2-0-fullx-module-targeting-premium-residential-solar-market/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Tongwei has unveiled the TNC 2.0 FULLX G12R-48, a TOPCon-based residential solar module that delivers 490W power output and 24.5% efficiency within the industry-standard 1762 × 1134 × 30mm format. It claims a 30W power gain over conventional TOPCon modules and an average 3.3% higher per-watt generation compared to back-contact \(BC\) modules, with innovations like a zero-cell-gap design and significantly reduced leakage current. The module is engineered to address residential challenges such as limited roof space, non-optimal orientations, and aesthetic demands, featuring Full Black options for seamless integration. Tongwei targets premium market segments, emphasizing benefits in areas with time-of-use pricing, complex roof layouts, and high-latitude regions with lower sunlight.

rss · PV-Tech · Oct 1, 08:11

**「Technical Details」** The module&\#x27;s core innovation is the 360° passivated TNC cell, which reduces defect losses and achieves over 26.3% cell efficiency. It employs louvred interconnection and invisible current-collection to create a zero-cell-gap design, reaching 95.1% cell density and lowering leakage current by over 50 times compared to conventional BC modules. These features enable improved low-light performance and reduced sensitivity to roof tilt, with leakage zones 63.7 times shorter than in BC modules.

**「Industry Impact」** The module is positioned to impact premium residential and niche markets, such as regions with strict aesthetic standards or time-of-use electricity pricing, by offering higher energy yields in suboptimal conditions. However, its broad industry effect may be limited as it represents an incremental improvement in TOPCon technology for residential applications.

### [Actis Launches 3GW Renewable Energy Platform in India](https://www.pv-tech.org/actis-launches-indian-renewable-energy-platform-3gw-of-capacity/) ⭐️ 5.0/10

Actis, a renewable energy investor, has launched Leo Energies, a new platform aiming to operate over 3GW of solar PV, onshore wind, and battery storage in India. The platform has already secured agreements to acquire approximately 650MW of operational solar PV capacity across five states and 50MWh of BESS. This launch capitalizes on India&\#x27;s booming solar sector, which saw 27GW of new PV capacity in the first half of the year, a 49% year-on-year increase. Leo Energies is Actis&\#x27; fourth renewables platform in India, building on previous successes like BluPine Energy which has exceeded 3GW capacity.
