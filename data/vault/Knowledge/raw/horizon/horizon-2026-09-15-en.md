### [CrossBoundary Energy commissions 233MW solar-plus-storage plant at DRC copper mine](https://www.pv-tech.org/crossboundary-energy-powers-up-233mw-solar-plus-storage-site-at-drc-copper-mine/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

CrossBoundary Energy has commenced commercial operations at a 233MW/526MWh solar-plus-storage facility located next to the Kamoa-Kakula copper mine in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. The project supplies round-the-clock power to the mine under a power purchase agreement and was built within sixteen months of signing the contract, which CrossBoundary described as the fastest construction timeline for a large-scale solar and battery project in Africa. The installation utilizes roughly 360,000 Aiko dual-glass ABC modules rated at 655W with a reported 24.2% efficiency to support continued expansion of the mining operations.

**「Technical Details」** The plant employs all-back-contact ABC cell architecture, which relocates both electrical contacts to the rear surface to reduce front-side shading and associated power losses. CrossBoundary cited the modules’ superior temperature coefficient and hotspot suppression as critical traits for maintaining safety and performance in the DRC’s hot climate. The 233MW photovoltaic array is paired with a 526MWh battery energy storage system to firm variable solar output and deliver dispatchable baseload power for heavy industrial loads.

**「Industry Impact」** By delivering firm, PPA-backed renewable baseload power to one of Africa’s largest copper mines, the project demonstrates that solar-plus-storage can now compete with and displace traditional fossil fuel generation in remote, energy-intensive extractive industries.

### [Spain launches 937MW renewables and storage tender in Teruel](https://www.pv-tech.org/spanish-government-seeks-937mw-renewables-and-energy-storage-in-teruel/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Spain’s Ministry of Ecological Transition \(MITECO\) has launched a procedure to award 937MW of grid access capacity for renewables and energy storage projects at the Mudéjar 400 kV substation in Teruel, northeastern Spain. The allocation is designed to replace a lignite-fired power plant exceeding 1GW located at the same site, with awards prioritized for more advanced projects that satisfy socioeconomic criteria and applications processed in order of receipt to accelerate the award timeline. For each megawatt secured, developers must hire one full-time worker, train one person for a minimum of 60 hours, provide 2kW of self-consumption capacity for local users, and invest €10,000 \(US$11,600\) in the Teruel value chain, all backed by a guarantee of €120 per installed kilowatt. The deadline to submit documentation is 22 September 2026.
