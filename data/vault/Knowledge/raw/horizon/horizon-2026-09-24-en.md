### [LONGi Sets HIBC Solar Cell Efficiency Record at 28.29%](https://www.pv-tech.org/longi-unveils-hibc-solar-cell-28-29-conversion-efficiency/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

LONGi has achieved a new company record of 28.29% conversion efficiency for its hybrid interdigitated back contact \(HIBC\) solar cell, certified by the Institute for Solar Energy Research Hamelin \(ISFH\). This marks the third time in 2024 the company has broken its own HIBC efficiency record, improving upon the previous 28.13% set in April. The new cells were showcased at an industry event in Romania and will be integrated into 700W modules from LONGi&\#x27;s &\#x27;EcoLife&\#x27; Hi-MO S10 and 700MW Hi-MO 9 series, which are now in mass production and available for purchase.

rss · PV-Tech · Sep 24, 11:46

**「Technical Details」** HIBC is a type of solar cell architecture that combines the advantages of heterojunction \(HJT\) technology with an interdigitated back contact \(IBC\) design. This structure moves all electrical contacts to the rear of the cell, eliminating shading losses on the front surface and enabling higher conversion efficiencies. The continuous efficiency improvements demonstrate advancements in cell processing and material quality for this back-contact technology.

**「Industry Impact」** This record reinforces LONGi&\#x27;s technological leadership and directly supports its commercial high-efficiency module portfolio, helping the company compete in a market increasingly focused on advanced technologies to navigate industry-wide financial pressures. The emphasis on back contact technology aligns with a broader industry trend toward higher-efficiency products, which may be further encouraged by new Chinese policies aimed at phasing out outdated technologies.

### [Spain initiates 11GW grid capacity allocation for renewables and storage](https://www.pv-tech.org/spain-launches-11gw-renewable-and-storage-grid-capacity-allocation-process/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Spain&\#x27;s Ministry for the Ecological Transition \(MITECO\) and the Institute for Just Transition \(ITJ\) have launched consultations to allocate up to 11GW of grid access capacity to renewable energy and storage projects in designated just transition areas. An initial Expression of Interest assessed demand at reserved grid nodes across regions including A Coruña, Almería, and Palencia. The process proposes two allocation methods: an accelerated, first-come-first-served procedure for 15 nodes and a competitive tender specifically for the Guardo 220kV and Velilla 400kV nodes due to significant interest in pumped hydro storage. Successful projects must meet socio-economic and environmental requirements. Stakeholder submissions are open until 8 October 2026.

rss · PV-Tech · Sep 24, 12:52

**「Technical details」** The allocation targets specific high-voltage grid nodes \(220kV and 400kV\) in regions historically dependent on fossil fuels. The two distinct procedures reflect technical and market conditions: a simplified competitive allocation for general renewable projects at most nodes, and a formal competitive tender for the Guardo-Velilla zone where the Expression of Interest identified pronounced demand for pumped hydro energy storage projects. Project eligibility is conditional on meeting environmental assessment requirements and making socio-economic commitments to the local just transition area.

**「Industry impact」** This allocation process directly accelerates renewable and storage deployment in strategic Spanish regions, potentially unlocking over 11GW of new capacity while supporting community transitions. The separate tender for Guardo-Velilla signals official recognition and prioritization of large-scale storage, likely benefiting pumped hydro developers and reinforcing grid flexibility goals following recent awards like the 2.3GWh of storage under the RENOINN 2 programme.

### [ARENA Funds AU$25M Lightspeed Trial to Cut Solar Construction Costs](https://www.pv-tech.org/australias-arena-backs-au25-million-lightspeed-trial-to-cut-construction-costs-at-solar-sites/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

The Australian Renewable Energy Agency \(ARENA\) has committed AU$25 million to Equans Solar &amp; Storage for a three-year Lightspeed trial testing up to 10 technologies in five categories—civil works, mechanical works, electrical works, O&amp;M, and digital coordination—to reduce the levelised cost of electricity \(LCOE\) for utility-scale solar. This initiative addresses stalled balance-of-system costs in Australia, which constitute the dominant expense despite declining module prices, with weighted-average installed costs at AU$1.52 per watt as reported in an ARENA white paper. The first technology deployed is the Nextpower Ranger autonomous inspection robot, marking its first use in an Australian solar farm. The trial builds on ARENA&\#x27;s portfolio approach, similar to Fortescue’s Pilbara Solar Innovation Hub, and supports the agency&\#x27;s &\#x27;30-30-30&\#x27; vision targeting 30% module efficiency, 30 cents per watt installed cost, and LCOE below AU$20/MWh by 2030.

rss · PV-Tech · Sep 24, 03:51

**「Technical Details」** The trial specifically targets balance-of-system costs, which include labour, civil works, grid connection, and project delivery and have remained resistant to reduction. Technologies being tested span autonomous inspection robots like the Nextpower Ranger, advanced piling and tracking systems, and digital coordination tools to improve productivity in construction and operations. The emphasis is on automation to address inefficiencies in project delivery, aiming to validate and scale emerging innovations in real-world environments.

**「Industry Impact」** This trial is expected to significantly reduce the levelised cost of electricity for utility-scale solar in Australia, directly supporting ARENA&\#x27;s goal to halve generation costs to around AU$25-30/MWh and unlock 10GW of annual capacity additions. If successful, it could accelerate the adoption of automation in solar construction and O&amp;M, influencing industry practices and aiding Australia&\#x27;s renewable energy build-out towards net zero.

### [US Solar Policy Shifts: Equipment Bans and Tariffs](https://www.pv-tech.org/us-policy-focus-india-ad-cvd-power-equipment-ban-both-overshadowed-by-bigger-policy-challenges/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

The US has enacted several solar policy changes, including an executive order on August 26 banning imports of power equipment like inverters, transformers, and battery energy storage systems from 24 countries, with China being a key target, while the Department of Commerce finalized anti-dumping and countervailing duty rates for Indian solar cells at 123.04% and 126.09%, respectively. These security-focused measures, alongside Trump administration policies, have contributed to nearly 40,000 clean energy job losses in 2025 and threaten to increase costs and slow renewable energy deployment. Additionally, a Rhode Island judge ruled the cancellation of the Solar For All program unlawful, offering some relief for low-income consumers.
