### [First Solar to withdraw ITC TOPCon complaint, continue patent lawsuits](https://www.pv-tech.org/first-solar-to-withdraw-section-337-complaint-on-topcon-ip-continues-us-district-court-lawsuits/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

First Solar plans to voluntarily withdraw its US International Trade Commission Section 337 complaint alleging tunnel oxide passivated contact \(TOPCon\) intellectual property violations, while continuing separate patent lawsuits in US District Court against Jinko, Canadian Solar, T1 Energy, and Trina Solar that had been stayed pending the ITC investigation. The cadmium telluride thin-film manufacturer said the Trump Administration’s newly imposed Section 232 tariffs on polysilicon help level the playing field at the border, and it therefore intends to focus on enforcing its global TOPCon patent portfolio—which it acquired via the 2013 TetraSun purchase and holds through 2030 and beyond—through domestic litigation. The company, which licensed its TOPCon IP to Talon PV last year, anticipates filing additional infringement suits and characterized the ITC withdrawal as a procedural decision to clear the way for those pending and future cases.

**「Industry impact」** The ongoing uncertainty from First Solar’s TOPCon patent enforcement has influenced technology investment in the US solar cell sector, where currently operational capacity remains dominated by PERC even though announced factory plans are roughly split among PERC \(11.8 GW\), TOPCon \(13.1 GW\), and HJT \(12.2 GW\).
