### [REPowerEU less than 20% funded, EU solar growth stalls](https://www.pv-tech.org/repowereu-is-faltering-with-less-than-20-of-investment-realised-says-brussels-court/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

The European Court of Auditors reported that as of April 2026, EU member states had committed only €54.3 billion, or less than one-fifth, of the €300 billion REPowerEU funding available to reduce dependence on Russian gas and accelerate renewables. Most National Energy and Climate Plans lacked specific actions to advance the plan’s objectives, and the additional renewable capacity created under the scheme remains negligible and far below the 103GW target. Solar PV growth stalled in 2025 for the first time in a decade amid overcrowded grid queues, price cannibalisation, and regulatory hurdles for integrating storage, with trade association SolarPower Europe not expecting a return to 2024 market levels until almost the end of the decade.

### [Georgia Tech demonstrates 24.3% efficient copper-contacted TOPCon cells](https://www.pv-tech.org/georgia-tech-researchers-copper-paste-topcon-solar-cells-24-3-conversion-efficiency/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Researchers from the Georgia Institute of Technology demonstrated n-type tunnel oxide passivated contact \(TOPCon\) solar cells using a prototype copper paste that achieved a maximum conversion efficiency of 24.33% and an average of 24.14% through standard industrial screen-printing and firing equipment. Both copper and silver paste cells required laser-enhanced contact optimization \(LECO\) to surpass 24% efficiency, as unoptimized versions of both remained below 20%. Although silver-contacted control cells reached marginally higher efficiencies up to 24.54%, the copper cells compensated for higher contact resistivity by increasing metal coverage from 7% to 18%, a trade-off considered viable because silver averages roughly 145 times the price of copper. The study, published in Solar Energy by PhD student Ruohan Zhong with paste supplied by Bert Thin Films, was independently validated by the National Laboratory of the Rockies, which recorded 24.4% efficiency for copper and 24.6% for silver.

rss · PV-Tech · Sep 15, 10:49

**「Technical details」** The TOPCon cells employed screen-printed metallization using Bert Thin Films’ prototype copper paste, which can be processed on standard industrial equipment but necessitated a larger contact coverage area—18% compared to 7% for silver—to offset copper’s higher contact resistivity. Laser-enhanced contact optimization \(LECO\) proved essential to achieving competitive efficiencies above 24%, since both metallization types performed below 20% without this step. The comparable performance between copper and silver under LECO treatment indicates that contact resistivity limitations can be managed within existing cell architectures.

**「Industry impact」** The demonstration establishes copper paste as a credible, near-parity alternative to silver for screen-printed n-type TOPCon metallization, offering manufacturers a practical route to reduce contact material costs and silver supply dependence without retrofitting mainstream production equipment. Independent validation by the National Laboratory of the Rockies supports the reliability of the results for industrial translation.

### [SolarPower Europe says NZIA too slow to revive EU solar manufacturing](https://www.pv-tech.org/nzia-too-slow-to-revive-european-solar-manufacturing-says-solarpower-europe/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

SolarPower Europe reports that the Net Zero Industry Act \(NZIA\) has been implemented too slowly and too fragmentedly to revive European solar PV manufacturing, with only six EU member states incorporating its criteria into renewable energy auctions so far. The NZIA mandates that public procurement and capacity auctions allocate a portion of contracts to products from &quot;resilient&quot; non-dominant supplier countries, but divergent national approaches are creating additional complexity for market participants instead of leveraging the EU Single Market. The European Solar Manufacturing Council noted that member states remain reluctant because Italy&\#x27;s NZIA auctions did not favor European products, prompting both groups to back stricter &quot;Made in Europe&quot; criteria under the Industrial Accelerator Act \(IAA\). SolarPower Europe deputy CEO Dries Acke called the IAA a &quot;watershed moment&quot; and urged the European Commission to harmonize implementation while establishing a Cleantech Manufacturing Bank with production-linked financial support under the European Competitiveness Fund.

**「Industry Impact」** The fragmented NZIA rollout across just six member states leaves European solar manufacturers without sufficient demand certainty to compete with subsidized production in the US and India, intensifying pressure on Brussels to establish production-linked financing via a proposed Cleantech Manufacturing Bank.

### [Brazil adds 891MW utility-scale solar in August 2026](https://www.pv-tech.org/brazil-installs-891mw-utility-scale-solar-pv-in-august/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Brazil connected 891MW of new utility-scale solar PV in August 2026, pushing the year-to-date total to 3.5GW and already surpassing the 2.8GW added during all of 2025, according to National Electricity Agency \(ANEEL\) data. Solar PV comprised nearly half of the roughly 2GW of new utility-scale capacity commissioned that month and 15 of the 20 new power plants entering operation, while cumulative utility-scale solar reached 23.6GW by early September. Trade body ABSOLAR reported 76.6GW of total installed solar when including distributed generation, which remains the larger segment, and 70.8GW of solar projects remain under development representing 81% of capacity in that pipeline. The strong deployment comes despite growing curtailment concerns, with Aurora Energy Research forecasting a 12% increase for renewable plants by 2032 driven by a government mandate for 2.5GW of new gas-fired power plants.

### [Official Q&amp;A clarifies China&\#x27;s mandatory national PV standards](https://www.pv-tech.org/official-qa-clarifies-rules-of-chinas-mandatory-pv-standards/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

China&\#x27;s Ministry of Industry and Information Technology issued five mandatory national standards—GB 47739-2026, GB 47740-2026, GB 47834-2026, GB 29447-2026, and GB 47835-2026—in May and June 2026 to regulate PV module safety, labelling, energy consumption, and efficiency, with energy-related rules taking effect on 1 January 2027 and safety/labelling rules on 1 June 2027. The accompanying official Q&amp;A clarifies that all domestically sold and deployed PV products must comply regardless of production date, while exempting R&amp;D samples and export-bound goods unless later resold in China. For end products, GB 47834-2026 sets minimum module efficiencies of 23.2% for TOPCon and HJT and 23.5% for back contact modules, plus bifaciality minimums of 75% for TOPCon, 85% for HJT, and 70% for BC modules, alongside graded inverter efficiency thresholds. The standards also mandate nameplate power deviation limits, require QR code links to remain accessible for a product&\#x27;s entire service life, and hold all sold inventory—including Grade B modules—to mandatory oversight once enforcement begins.

rss · PV-Tech · Sep 15, 09:30

**「Technical Details」** The energy consumption standard for monocrystalline silicon \(GB 47835-2026\) applies a 0.65 secondary conversion coefficient to half-cut wafers rather than assuming proportional reduction, reflecting additional processing energy. For modules, compliance requires meeting both conversion efficiency floors and environmental degradation resistance benchmarks, with energy efficiency ratings classified into three grades. Inverter compliance is determined by power-rating-specific minimums for weighted-average and maximum conversion efficiency, while nameplate traceability must be fully linked to national measurement benchmarks to ensure laboratory consistency.

**「Industry Impact」** The rules will force manufacturers to clear non-compliant inventory before enforcement dates and accelerate phase-out of low-efficiency inverters and substandard modules by establishing legally enforceable efficiency floors and unified national testing traceability.

### [US community solar firms raise $370m for 200MW in New York and Illinois](https://www.pv-tech.org/us-community-solar-ventures-raise-us370m-for-200mw-of-new-capacity/) ⭐️ 5.0/10

U.S. community solar developers 38 Degrees North and Generate Capital have closed over US$370 million in combined financing to fund nearly 200MW of new projects across New York and Illinois. 38 Degrees North secured a US$260 million facility from Apterra Infrastructure Capital to construct 85MW of community solar, while Generate Capital closed a US$117 million term debt facility with MUFJ for its 114MWdc Solar Fund 11 comprising 18 installations. These transactions bring 38 Degrees North’s total raised capital to approximately US$1.7 billion and reflect continued investor appetite for permitted, ready-to-build community solar pipelines in two of the country’s most active state programs.

**「Industry Impact」** The deals underscore sustained institutional confidence in late-stage U.S. community solar development, directing hundreds of millions in capital toward developers with shovel-ready pipelines in New York and Illinois even as interconnection queues lengthen nationally.
