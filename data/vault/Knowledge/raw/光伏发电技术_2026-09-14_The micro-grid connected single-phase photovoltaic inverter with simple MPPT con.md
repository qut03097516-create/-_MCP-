# The micro-grid connected single-phase photovoltaic inverter with simple MPPT controller

## 摘要
A photovoltaic (PV) source is reliable and emission free. The benefits of the micro-grid include high reliability. Therefore, a micro-grid connected PV system is a feasible source for electricity. However, nowadays the efficiency of a PV array is remains low. In the proposed MPPT algorithm, it was very simple under changing light intensity by only regulating the photovoltaic voltage. The control method was designed to make a sinusoidal current with minimal harmonics distortions, and also the unity power factor to be supplied to the micro-grid too. The performance of the proposed micro-grid connected single-phase photovoltaic inverter with simple MPPT controller was confirmed by simulation.

## 核心要点
- 光伏电源可靠且无排放，微电网具备高可靠性，微电网并网光伏系统是可行电源方案，但 PV 阵列效率仍偏低。
- 提出简单 MPPT 算法：随光照强度变化仅通过调节光伏电压实现最大功率跟踪。
- 控制方法使并网电流为正弦且谐波畸变最小，并向微电网提供单位功率因数。
- 所提微电网并网单相光伏逆变器 + 简单 MPPT 控制器的性能经仿真验证。
