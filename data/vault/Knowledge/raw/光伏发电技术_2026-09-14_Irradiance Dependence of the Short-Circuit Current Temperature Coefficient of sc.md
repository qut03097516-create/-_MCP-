## 摘要
Temperature coefficient of short-circuit current ( TCISC) of PV modules plays an important role in PV modeling and I-V characteristic correction. Many standards and researches have indicated that TCISC is irradiance-dependent, but the detailed relationship is not really clarified. Thus, this paper investigates the relationship of module TCISC with irradiance from both indoor and outdoor tests and reveal the impact of the varying TCISC in common applications.
